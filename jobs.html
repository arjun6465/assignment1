Senior Software Developer

Ref Code: AC130

Description: We are seeking an experienced Senior Software Developer to join our dynamic team and take the lead in designing and developing high-quality software solutions. This role involves working on complex projects, mentoring junior team members, and contributing to key technical decisions across the development lifecycle. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in software engineering, a passion for clean code, and the ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams.

Salary Range: $110,000 - $135,000 AUD

Reports to:

  • CTO (Chief Technology Officer)
  • Software Development Manager

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, test, and maintain high-quality software applications
  • Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve complex software issues
  • Ensure applications meet performance, security, and scalability requirements
  • Mentor junior developers and support their technical growth

Qualifications

Essential

  • Bachelors Degree in Computer science or Software Engineering
  • Strong proficiency in Java, Python and C#
  • 10+ years of Experience

Preferable

  • Experience with Agile/Scrum methodologies
  • Ability to lead and mentor other developers

Position 2: Junior Software Developer

Ref Code: 1738AY

Description: We are looking for an enthusiastic and motivated Junior Software Developer to join our development team. This is an entry-level position ideal for recent graduates or those with 1–2 years of experience who are passionate about building software and eager to learn and grow in a supportive environment. You’ll work closely with senior developers and other team members to design, develop, test, and maintain software solutions. This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on experience in the full development life cycle while working on real-world applications.

Salary Range: $60,000 - $75,000 AUD

Reports To: Senior Software Developer

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist in the development, testing, and deployment of web and software applications
  • Write clean, maintainable, and well-documented code
  • Follow company coding and security standards
  • Fix bugs and contribute to regular software updates

Qualifications

Essential

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering
  • Familiarity with at least one programming language (e.g., Python, Java, or C#)
  • Strong understanding of programming fundamentals and logic
  • Eagerness to learn and take feedback
  • Understanding of basic version control (e.g., Git)

Preferable

  • Portfolio or GitHub profile with sample projects
  • Experience using task tracking tools (e.g., Jira)
